## Data status

This release contains **pseudonymized and numerically perturbed demonstration data**（名称替换、数值扰动后的演示数据）adapted from existing source records. Names and identifiers were replaced, continuous measurements were perturbed, and the published full-model predictions and results were freshly computed from the transformed inputs. Binary labels, categorical indicators, group relationships, dilution concentrations and discrete comparison MIC values were retained.

These materials are not original measured data, a paper reproduction dataset, independently generated synthetic data or evidence of clinical performance. Name replacement and numerical perturbation do not guarantee anonymity or prevent linkage. The public package excludes source identity mappings, perturbation rules, original-value comparisons and private research audits.
